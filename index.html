Recommendation

Start with Jersey City

Jersey City has the most demand by a wide margin: at least 1,530 Google searches a month for drain and sewer help and 4,328 for plumbing overall, about 255 for each drain or plumbing company BBB lists within 3 miles. It also has the best offices: CoLab by DVORA has hosted reception and a 1-month minimum from $765 a month, 0.3 miles from City Hall.

Trade-offs: Jersey City is about 17 miles from the Haledon shop. Passaic is closest (about 6 miles) but has the least demand and the most competition, with 47 companies within 3 miles. North Bergen sits in between and has no coworking center of its own.

How this was put together

Business. NJ Drain & Plumbing Service LLC, (201) 275-4667, 325 Haledon Ave, Haledon, NJ, from the onboarding form on monday.com. Drain cleaning, sewer cleaning, plumbing and HVAC, 24/7.

Searches. Google Keyword Planner, November 24, 2026, average month from November 2025 to October 2026, using seven seed terms (plumber, plumbing, drain cleaning, sewer cleaning, clogged drain, sewer line repair, hydro jetting) for each town as Google defines it. Only service searches were kept (for example “drain cleaning near me”, “sewer line repair”, “emergency plumber”); do-it-yourself, product, brand and other-town searches were dropped, and searches Google treats as the same were counted once. Google gives a range for each keyword, so the figures are the lowest each total could be. Google’s own reach for its Jersey City and North Bergen areas (1.3 million and 1.42 million people) is far larger than for Passaic (139,000), so those two cover more of the surrounding area.

Competitors. BBB listings in the Plumber, Sewer Cleaning and Drain Cleaning categories, searched around each town and nearby ZIP codes, counting New Jersey companies whose name or main category is plumbing, drain or sewer work and that give a street address. Addresses were placed with the U.S. Census geocoder; each company counts once per town. BBB lists only businesses with a profile, so these are floors.

Offices. Coworking and small private offices within about 5 miles of each center, from LiquidSpace, Office Hub, CommercialSearch, CoworkingCafe and operator sites, checked November 24, 2026. Green is confirmed by the listing, amber is a question for the call, red fails. Google requires a business address to be staffed during its posted hours, so a front desk matters.
